Category System 课程

  1. Category 1

    Has attended a ground school and has successfully completed the syllabus requirements as detailed in Section 5 (Training), Paragraph 2 (The Basic Training System Syllabus), subpart 2.1 – 2.3 of the British Skydiving Operations Manual.

  2. Category 2

    a. Has demonstrated the ability to fall in a stable position counting throughout.

    b. Student Skydivers may transfer to the AFF method of training, at Level 2 once they have completed at least 2 jumps and have obtained Category 2.

  3. Category 3

    a. Has demonstrated the ability to perform three consecutive stable Dummy Ripcord Pulls (DRPs), counting and maintaining a positive arch throughout.

    b. Progression to free fall will only take place after a minimum of five static line descents and will only be authorised by at least a Category System Instructor, who must satisfy him/herself that the criteria laid down for progression has been attained. The first free fall descent will take place no later than the day following the last of the successful DRP descents.

  4. Category 4 a. Has demonstrated the ability to perform two consecutive stable delayed openings, counting throughout, of between three and five seconds.

    b. Category 4 descents must take place from a minimum altitude of 4,000ft AGL.

  5. Category 5

    a. Has demonstrated the ability to perform two consecutive stable delayed openings of ten seconds, maintaining a heading and counting throughout.

    b. Student Skydivers may transfer to the AFF method of training, at level 3 once they have obtained Category 5.

  6. Category 6

    a. Has demonstrated the ability to perform the following:

    b. Two consecutive delayed openings of fifteen seconds.

    Use an altimeter in free fall.

    c. Upon successful completion of Category 6, Student Skydivers may be converted to ‘throwaway’ pilot chutes. For at least the first 2 conversion jumps no further freefall progression may take place.

  7. Category 7

    a. Has demonstrated the ability to perform controlled 360° turns in both directions.

    b. Category 7 descents must take place from a minimum altitude of 6,000ft AGL.

  8. Category 8

    Has demonstrated the ability to perform the following:

    a. An unstable exit.

    b. A dive exit.

    c. Back loops.

    d. Tracks, including a track turn as an avoidance manoeuvre.

    e. No tracking exercises may be commenced below an exit altitude of 7,000ft AGL.

    f. A back loop, 360° left turn, 360° right turn, a short track and wave off, from a minimum altitude of 8,000ft AGL.

2023 -06-25

  • Jp. 8.
  • 10 s delay

状态

  • 出舱后，前五秒平衡但是随后失去平衡，随后两次恢复平衡但很快失去平衡。最后在12秒左右开伞。高度2100。
  • 降落区过窄，最后阶段高度300以下下没有足够空间。差点撞高压线树和栅栏。

改进

  • 出舱动作别向右转
  • 保持 arch 直到平衡，开始五秒会不稳定，手臂伸直保持 arch 直到平衡
  • 寻找 open space

2023 -07-30

  • Jp. 10
  • 10 s delay

状态

Freefall

完美，10-12秒 delay，完全稳定没有旋转。

UnderCanopy

开伞后由于地面标志物类似机场建筑，所以以为在机场上方，直到2500ft 左右反复确认了三四次，因为没有跑道，才反应过来不在机场上空，期间做了2-3圈 full turn，掉到了2000ft 左右的高度。用 flat-turn 找了大概4分钟都没有找到 dropzone 在哪里，已经做好了降落在田里的准备。最终在很远的方向上找到了 dropzone 的跑道。此时高度不太理想了，用 half-drive 朝机场飞去，同时也是 extended range 训练。从机场的西边一直飞到的东边，大脑没有反应过来，所以不敢转弯，其实此时高度完全可以，飞到机场中央时候处于大概500ft 左右。最终落在了机场东边的一片麦田里，压到了3m 左右的麦子。

  • 准备开始像 Sam 一样记录每次在 Strathallan 发生的点点滴滴，以后再看将是一份完美的回忆。

改进

  • 暂时没有，只是 fun jump

2023 -08-27

  • Jp. 15
  • TURNS (8000 ft)

状态

Freefall

  • 出舱后，5秒钟内后仰改平，nice arch。
  • 身体开始左右摇摆，很困难的保持 stable。开始不控制的轻微右旋 (7000 ft)
  • 继续旋转加速，主要是身体一直摇摆，没有 flip，但是控制力很差。(6000 ft)
  • 加速旋转，开始尝试停下，但是旋转速度太快，向左倾大概45度才勉强停下来。(5000 ft)
  • 准备 pull the handle，但是又抓 altimeter。更不稳了
  • 拔 handle，伞完全打开时候正好卡在3000ft 左右，低于要求开伞高度。

B license

  • JM1 - spotting; Learn how we decide the jump run, how to assess the landing direction and how to safely check the 'spot' for your fellow jumpers
  • JM1 - flightline checking; learn how to check your fellow jumpers' equipment is safe to use, regulations around what equipment different jumpers can use and how to keep a record of checks at Langar
  • JM1 - Introduction to the Ops Manual; You'll learn all about what's in the Operations Manual and how it applies to you and your fellow jumpers
  • JM1 - being the jumpmaster; how to plan a load, deciding the jump order, taking action in an emergency and being responsible for your fellow jumpers
  • CT1 - developing your skills as a canopy pilot, you'll learn more about how your canopy works, why it does what it does, safety considerations as you develop and the practical exercises you need to use to learn new canopy skills
